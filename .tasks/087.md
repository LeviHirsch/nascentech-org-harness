A person coming to exist on the box should be a recorded event, not a side effect of a mkdir. Registration happens once; every session after is routing to an existing user tree.

Depends on the action log (T11) — there is nowhere to record the event until that exists.

Supersedes the literal reading of T2 ('mkdir users/<slug>/'), which shipped as a self-healing mkdir in clv-login. That stays as the v0 until this lands.
