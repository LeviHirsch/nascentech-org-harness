Removing someone (T103) leaves two gaps:
1. A removed person's Cloudflare session can last up to a week unless it's revoked in the Zero Trust dashboard.
2. Tailscale and LAN connections reach SSH directly, so the person's authorized_keys line must come out too.

Ruled (Levi 10/2): "fine with the two gaps … for now (worth being on the radar as potential things for later but also can be resurfaced pretty easily)".

Intent: removing someone actually ends their access.

Later (Levi 10/3): "eventually we'll need to automate the removal of their registration but that is a later thing." Today removal is by hand: Levi runs nt-register --remove <name>, and the person runs clv uninstall --all. Automating it means one action that takes the key line off the mini when someone is removed from Google Workspace or uninstalls.
