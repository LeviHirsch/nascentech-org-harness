On the mini every session is uid 502, so $HOME/.collevity/user/ is ONE directory shared by everyone. clv resolve_identity reads $CLV_HOME/user/subject whenever a principal is set, without checking the subject belongs to that principal. The moment anyone declares a subject there, every other principal inherits it — levi would act as sabrina's Google subject.

Violates the AGENTS.md invariant that the harness refuses to act as a Google subject other than whoami.

Latent right now, not live: found 2026-08-16 during T3 while testing clv-stamp; ~/.collevity/user/ on the mini currently holds no principal or subject file. It becomes real the first time declared identity is used (T60).

Shipped (2026-08-16): Subjects are now keyed by principal: clv reads $CLV_HOME/user/<principal>/subject instead of the shared $CLV_HOME/user/subject. Demonstrated the leak against the old binary — principal=ben with a subject file written by someone else returned subject=victim@nascentech.com; now returns empty. The legacy shared path is never read, and its presence prints a warning to stderr (not silence: a subject that quietly evaporates is as bad as one that quietly leaks; stderr keeps whoami --json parseable). CLV_SUBJECT override and method=none behaviour unchanged. clv-dwd was checked and takes its subject as an explicit argument, so it did not carry a second copy of the bug.
