Built 10/3 as clv login, with nt as its one-word shortcut (LeviHirsch/homebrew-tap, clv/). It shows a banner going in ("Connecting to the NascenTech server") and coming out ("Back on your own computer"), and sets the window title while on the server. With arguments, or without a terminal, it behaves exactly like plain ssh nt. Levi got in with nt from his MacBook Air on 10/3, as airtest.

When a login fails it says why and what to do: not registered yet (run clv key and text the line to Levi); an old record of the server (run clv update); offline; the connection dropped; the browser sign-in didn't finish.

Left: the server being down (T97) has no message of its own, and on Windows the reasons are coarse (offline, or "not registered or sign-in not finished"). "Key isn't loaded" no longer applies while the key has no passphrase (T153).

Done when: a registered person gets in with one word, and each failure shows its own message, the server being down included.

Intent: login is one terminal command, and a non-coder knows what to do when it doesn't work.
