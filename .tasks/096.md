Why it matters: after any power cut the mini waits at the disk-unlock screen until someone types its password in person, and nobody can sign in remotely until then. That happened 8/31–9/3 and again around 9/23. The cause is FileVault, the Mac's disk encryption, which protects the server's files and its Google key if the machine is stolen.

Options:
- keep FileVault, add a battery backup (UPS), and name who unlocks it and how fast;
- turn FileVault off so the server comes back by itself, accepting that a stolen mini exposes the Google key and the shared files.

What it unblocks: T97.

Intent: the server is there when people need it, at a level of risk Levi chooses.
